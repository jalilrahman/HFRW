FAQs

Questions, answered.

How does the programme work?

Twelve weeks, one topic a week. Each week you study real brands, analyse a commercial decision and complete a practical task you can submit for feedback. Weekly live seminars and personal mentoring sit alongside.

Who is it for?

People on the commercial side of fashion and luxury, from graduates to managers aiming for Head of or Director, plus designers and founders who want to make better business decisions.

How much time do I need?

Up to 3 hours a week. Mentoring is extra, so use as much or as little as you need.

What does it cost?

$49 a month on Skool. Cancel any time.

Do I get direct access to Jalil?

Yes. Mentoring is built in from day one. Use it for a programme task, a problem at work or your next career move.

Do I need a finance background?

No. Everything is built step by step from where most fashion professionals start.

Will it get me promoted?

It gives you the skills, the evidence and the confidence to go after it. It can't guarantee a promotion.

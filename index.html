FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is How Fashion Really Works?

HFRW is a 12-week programme that teaches you how fashion brands make money and how senior leaders make decisions. It comes with weekly live seminars and personal mentoring from a fashion Chief Commercial Officer.

Who is it for?

It's for ambitious professionals on the commercial side of fashion and luxury: buyers, merchandisers, marketers, product developers, ecommerce, licensing and wholesale teams, and founders. Most members are managers working towards Head of or Director.

What will I learn?

You'll learn how the whole system works, not just one function. The twelve weeks run in this order:

  • The fashion business foundations
  • Why customers buy
  • Positioning, branding and competitive advantage
  • Product strategy and commercial creativity
  • Retail, wholesale and distribution
  • Revenue growth beyond product
  • Commercial literacy and financial fluency
  • Scaling a fashion business
  • Culture, influence and relevance
  • How fashion companies actually operate
  • Career paths in the fashion business
  • Thinking like an executive

Along the way you'll learn to use AI for the analysis without outsourcing the decision.

How is the programme structured?

Twelve weeks, one theme a week. Each week you study major brands, analyse real commercial decisions and complete a practical task you can submit for feedback.

How much time does it take?

No more than 3 hours a week. Mentoring is extra, so you can use as much or as little of it as you need.

Is it recorded or live?

Both. The weekly lessons and seminar library are recorded, so you can fit them around your job. The weekly live seminars and mentoring are where you ask questions and bring real problems.

Do I get direct access to Jalil?

Yes. Mentoring is built in from day one. Use it for a programme task, a problem at work or your next career move.

Do I need a finance background?

No. Everything is built step by step from where most fashion professionals start. You do need to do the exercises.

Is this an MBA alternative?

For fashion, yes. An MBA teaches general business over two years and costs tens of thousands. HFRW teaches the commercial decisions specific to fashion and luxury, from a working CCO, for $49 a month.

Will it get me promoted?

It gives you the skills, the evidence and the confidence to go after a promotion. It can't guarantee one. That depends on your starting point, your effort and the openings around you.

Can I start any time, and cancel?

Yes. You can start the programme the day you join, and membership is monthly, so you can cancel any time.

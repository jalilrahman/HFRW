Fit

Is HFRW right for you?

HFRW is for you if

  • You work, or want to work, on the commercial side of fashion: buying, merchandising, marketing, product, ecommerce, licensing, wholesale, retail, or as a founder
  • You run your own area well but want to understand how the whole business works
  • You want Head of, Director or beyond within the next one to three years
  • You'll apply what you learn at work and act on feedback

HFRW is not for you if

  • You only want a certificate or passive video content
  • You expect a guaranteed promotion or title
  • You're after design or creative training. HFRW covers the commercial side
  • You won't practise or use it on real work
FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is How Fashion Really Works?

HFRW builds the commercial fluency you need to succeed in the modern fashion industry. It starts with 14 core modules, then moves into a structured 12-week programme, with live seminars and direct mentoring from a working CCO alongside.

Who is it for?

It's for ambitious professionals on the commercial side of fashion and luxury: buyers, merchandisers, marketers, product developers, ecommerce, licensing and wholesale teams, and founders. Most members are managers working towards Head of or Director.

What will I learn?

You'll learn how the whole system works, not just one function. The 14 core modules cover how brands create value, how pricing protects profitability, supply chains, wholesale and distribution, licensing, how organisations scale, and how executives make decisions.

The 12-week programme then puts those foundations to work: why customers buy, how culture drives demand and how leaders navigate uncertainty. It also shows you how to use AI for analysis without outsourcing the decision.

How is the programme structured?

There are two stages. First come the 14 self-paced core modules. Then the 12-week programme takes one theme a week: you study major brands, analyse real commercial decisions and complete a practical task you can submit for feedback.

Is it recorded or live?

Both. The modules and seminar library are recorded, so you can fit them around your job. The weekly live seminars and mentoring are where you ask questions and bring real problems.

Do I get direct access to Jalil?

Yes. Mentoring is built in from day one. Use it for a programme task, a problem at work or your next career move.

Do I need a finance background?

No. Everything is built step by step from where most fashion professionals start. You do need to do the exercises.

Is this an MBA alternative?

For fashion, yes. An MBA teaches general business over two years and costs tens of thousands. HFRW teaches the commercial decisions specific to fashion and luxury, from a working CCO, for $49 a month.

Will it get me promoted?

It gives you the skills, the evidence and the confidence to go after a promotion. It can't guarantee one. That depends on your starting point, your effort and the openings around you.

Can I start any time, and cancel?

Yes. You can start the core modules the day you join, and membership is monthly, so you can cancel any time.
